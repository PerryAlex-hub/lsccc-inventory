## Design Context

### Users

LSCCC staff and administrators manage equipment, receipts, custody, repairs, and invite-only account access. They use desktop and mobile screens for daily operational work.

### Brand Personality

Official, calm, clear. The user chose invite-only staff access with a calm, official LSCCC look. The app uses the LSCCC crest; the user has now supplied a public logo URL for account emails.

### Aesthetic Direction

Continue the established light surfaces, navy primary color, muted green accents, and readable typography. Account emails should resemble official correspondence, with an LSCCC wordmark, clear next steps, and restrained decoration. Email rendering and readable fallbacks take precedence over custom web fonts or advanced layout features.

### Design Principles

1. Identify LSCCC clearly with its crest and text branding, including a readable fallback when email clients block images.
2. State the action, access implications, and link expiry in plain language.
3. Make forms and account-management controls usable on small screens and with keyboards.
4. Confirm permanent account deletion and explain what happens to operational history.
5. Keep permission enforcement on the server as well as in the interface.
